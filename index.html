Why Bukit Bintang, Why Pavilion Square

The last piece of land bridged directly into Pavilion KL.

Buyers today are choosing between KLCC, TRX and Bukit Bintang. Bukit Bintang is Kuala Lumpur's original Golden Triangle address — Malaysia's answer to Orchard Road and Ginza — and Pavilion Square is the only residential-and-office development with a private bridge straight into the mall.

01

Private link bridge into Pavilion KL — any weather, safe, direct to 1.6 million sq ft of luxury retail, dining and entertainment.

02

Surrounded by 5-star hotels — Pullman, Banyan Tree, JW Marriott, The Ritz-Carlton, The Westin and Dorsett Regency — an address that impresses guests and clients.

03

Nearest integrated address to Prince Court Medical Centre, unlike the TRX masterplan which has no hospital within its footprint.

04

Pavilion Square's own corporate suites fuel residential demand — the same investment logic that underpins TRX office towers driving TRX Residences.

The Location

Steps from everything Kuala Lumpur is known for.
